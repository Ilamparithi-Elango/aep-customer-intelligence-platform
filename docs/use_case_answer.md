The use case:

Imagine a large enterprise — say a retailer or healthcare company — that uses Adobe Experience Platform and runs an AI chatbot handling thousands of customer interactions every day across web, mobile, IVR, and kiosk channels.

The problem they face is three silos of data that nobody is connecting:
- The chatbot logs (what questions customers asked, did the bot answer confidently?)
- The web/app clickstream (did the customer bounce, how long did they stay, what pages did they visit before opening chat?)
- The session-level outcomes (was the customer satisfied? did they get escalated to a human agent? what was their CSAT score?)

Without a platform like this, a product manager who wants to answer "Why are customers on Mobile App escalating so much for Returns & Refunds in Healthcare?" has to go ask a data engineer to write a SQL query and wait a day.

What this platform does:

1. Unifies all three data sources daily into a single enriched dataset
2. Enriches with AI — GPT-4o-mini reads each interaction and adds sentiment, churn risk, issue classification, and a plain-English summary
3. Surfaces insights through a live dashboard — containment rate, escalation trends by topic, CSAT, thumbs feedback — all filterable by business unit, channel, and date
4. Answers questions in plain English — a PM can type "Which topic has the highest escalation rate for Healthcare this week?" and get an answer in seconds, no SQL needed

The bottom line for the business:

Every 1% improvement in containment rate (bots resolving issues without human escalation) saves a contact center roughly $X per 1,000 sessions in agent handling costs. This platform gives the team the visibility to find those opportunities — by topic, channel, and business unit — and act on them fast.
